SOLUTIONS 2K27 OFFICIAL REGULATIONS

SOLUTIONS 2K27 is the apex national inter-college technical festival of Army Institute of Technology, Pune. All registrations, team creations, problem statements, and round submissions are managed officially through Unstop.

REGISTER ON UNSTOP (OFFICIAL) VIEW 10 NATIONAL COMPETITIONS
📝

1. Registration & Team Formation

All teams must register through the official Unstop festival page. Inter-college team formation is permitted across tracks to foster nationwide collaboration. Team sizes vary between 1 to 4 members depending on the specific competition track.

🎓

2. Student Eligibility Criteria

Open to all currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students from recognized technical institutions and universities across India. A valid college student ID card is mandatory for offline campus entry and prize disbursements.

⚡

3. Hybrid Competition Rounds

Competitions consist of Round 1 (Online Prelims / Abstract / Code Submission on Unstop) followed by Round 2 (Offline Grand Finale at the AIT Pune campus). Shortlisted finalists will receive formal invitation letters for campus accommodation.

🛡️

4. Originality & Anti-Plagiarism

Submissions must represent original engineering work developed by the registered team. Use of pre-built commercial turnkey solutions or uncredited codebases will lead to immediate and irreversible disqualification by the technical jury.

⚖️

5. Fair Play & Judicial Integrity

Any tampering with tournament servers, interference with opponent hardware, or breach of the campus honor code will result in disqualification and notification to the participant's parent institution. All judge verdicts are final and binding.

📞

6. Coordination & Helpdesk

Official communications, schedule announcements, and shortlisted team notifications will be relayed through Unstop and registered emails. For festival grievances, contact the AIT Technical Board Secretariat via the Contact Directory.
