AIT TECHNICAL BOARD

The apex student technical organization of Army Institute of Technology, Pune. Spearheading technical learning, competitive engineering, open-source innovation, and cross-departmental collaboration under the motto "Engineering Ideas. Building Tomorrow."

OUR CORE PURPOSE

NURTURING ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

The Technical Board acts as the central catalyst for all technical student activities at AIT Pune. We bridge academic theory with high-stakes practical implementation, hosting national competitive hackathons, robotics arenas, algorithmic challenges, and hands-on workshops across emerging technologies.

⚡

COMPETITIVE ARENAS

Organizing high-impact national and intra-college competitions that challenge students to build, test, and deploy production-grade solutions.

🛠️

HANDS-ON LEARNING

Equipping aspiring engineers with real-world skills through deep-dive sessions in software engineering, embedded systems, AI/ML, and CAD.

🤝

INTER-BRANCH SYNERGY

Fostering intense cross-departmental collaboration among Computer, IT, Electronics & Telecommunication, and Mechanical/ARE students.

🌐

INDUSTRY COLLABORATION

Connecting students with leading tech titans, startup founders, and sponsors to build meaningful career trajectories and mentorship channels.

FLAGSHIP PLATFORMS

OUR TWO DISTINCTIVE TECHNICAL ECOSYSTEMS

The AIT Technical Board governs and orchestrates two major technical festivals, each serving a unique competitive purpose.

MAJOR INTER-COLLEGE FESTIVAL

SOLUTIONS 2K27

The flagship national-level technical festival of Army Institute of Technology, Pune. Bringing together leading student engineers, coders, and robot builders from colleges across India for competitive hackathons, robotics challenges, and cash prizes on Unstop.

  • National Inter-College Participation
  • Official Registrations on Unstop Portal
  • 10+ Verified National Competitions
  • Industry Sponsorship & Cash Prizes
