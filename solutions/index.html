ABOUT SOLUTIONS 2K27

Organized annually by the AIT Technical Board (guided by faculty mentors Dr. Ashwini Sapkal & Dr. P. B. Karandikar), SOLUTIONS stands as one of western India's largest national inter-college technical festivals with over 11,500 total participants and 11,000+ external engineers competing across India. The festival provides a national platform for aspiring engineers, researchers, and innovators to push algorithmic boundaries, solve industry-grade hardware challenges, and pitch high-impact ventures.

With participants travelling from premier technical institutes nationwide, SOLUTIONS 2K27 blends fierce technical competition with peer mentorship, technical workshops, and direct recognition from corporate tech leaders.

  • Fair, transparent, proctored evaluation hosted on Unstop
  • Industry-standard problem statements across AI, Cloud, Embedded Systems & CAD
  • Official certificates and national recognition from Army Institute of Technology, Pune

■ ELIGIBILITY & TEAMS

SOLUTIONS 2K27 is open to bona fide undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized university or engineering institution. Inter-college teams are permitted for team events. Valid student college identity cards are mandatory.

■ ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Zero tolerance for plagiarism, code unauthorized AI generation in unpermitted rounds, or foul play. Submissions are subjected to automated algorithmic similarity checkers. Infractions lead to immediate disqualification.

■ QUERIES & HELPLINE

For any queries regarding registration, accommodation, or problem statements, contact the AIT Technical Board student coordinators or post in the official Unstop discussion forum for rapid resolution.
