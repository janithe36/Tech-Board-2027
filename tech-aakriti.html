01 // INTERNAL ARENA

THE AIT TECHNICAL ARENA

Conceived as an intense departmental technical tournament, Tech Aakriti tests the depth and breadth of technical training across all engineering branches of Army Institute of Technology, Pune.

Each department fields its best coders, hardware builders, roboticists, debaters, and gamers. With normalized mass-participation formulas ensuring fair representation across differing department batch strengths, every single attendee contributes to their department's standing.

Coding & Software

3 Events

Algorithmic speed coding, blind debugging, and code-hunting challenges testing computer science fundamentals.

Core Engineering

3 Events

Analog circuit design, CAD assembly modeling, microcontroller debugging, and mechanical transmission design.

Mass Participation

2 Events

High-turnout departmental quizzes and showcases with formula-normalized branch attendance scoring.

Major Flagships

5 Events

Full-scale hackathons, robotics combat arenas, project exhibitions, and engineering prototype judging.

Open Category

2 Events

Cross-department tech paper presentations, technical policy debates, and innovation idea pitching.

Esports & Gaming

3 Events

Inter-branch tactical esports tournaments with official LAN brackets and department points awards.
